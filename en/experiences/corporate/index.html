Silent disco for a corporate event

A corporate party where nobody needs persuading to dance

Everyone has their own music. But the dance floor is one.

There is no need to look for music that the whole team will like.

In the headphones everyone picks their own rhythm. Some dance to familiar hits, some switch to electronic, and some find their own channel and simply let go.

And then the best part begins: people switch channels, show each other theirs, laugh and pick up their colleagues’ rhythm.

Everyone lives the evening in their own way. But nobody is left on the sidelines.

The team remembers a corporate party like this for a long time.

Fewer compromises. More freedom.

You gather the team. We create the experience.

Silent removes the usual limits of a corporate party, so all you have to do is enjoy the evening.

  1. Everyone finds their own rhythmThere is no need to convince everyone to listen to the same music. Everyone picks their own and still stays part of one shared party.
  2. Dance without the extra noiseThe music plays only in the headphones, so it bothers neither guests nor the venue. Silent can be held where an ordinary loud party would run into restrictions.
  3. The conversation does not end on the dance floorWant to talk? Just take the headphones off. Want to go back to the music? Put them on again. You do not have to choose between dancing and chatting.

How it works

Put on. Pick. Dance.

Three music channels, LED lights, no wires. We take care of the technical side, so you can enjoy your time with the team.

  1. Three music channelsEvery guest gets Silent Disco headphones and can switch between three channels, each with its own genre, playlist or mood.
  2. LED lightsThe colour of the headphones shows which channel a person is listening to. Spot your colour in the room and you know who is on the same wave.
  3. Wireless formatNo cables and no complicated installation for guests. Move wherever you like: the music follows you and sounds equally clean anywhere on the dance floor.
  4. Silent, fully managedWe prepare the equipment, set up the system and look after the technical side of the evening for your number of guests and location.

Corporate party entertainment: frequently asked questions about Silent Disco

How many headphones do we need and how much does it cost?

One for each participant: at least 40. The minimum set, 40 headphones for 4 hours, costs from 12,000 UAH including delivery, setup, support and pick-up. Extra headphones and hours are added to that amount, and the calculator below shows an estimate for your team straight away. Put the approximate number of guests in your request and we will advise on the best way to handle it.

What if colleagues feel shy about dancing?

For the first few minutes guests really do hold back, and that is normal. But nobody has to “go on stage” here: everyone has their own channel and their own volume, so you can simply listen and nod along, and joining those who are already dancing takes one touch of the headphones. Anyone who wants to talk takes them off around the neck, and the conversation goes on in a normal voice.

Where can it be held and what do you need from us?

Almost anywhere: in an office, a restaurant, a countryside complex or an open-air venue. From you we need space for dancing (roughly 1 m² per person if everyone dances) and a power socket nearby; the rest we bring and take away ourselves, and we need about 30 minutes before the start to set up and check the signal. There are no speakers, so volume is usually not an issue with the landlord or neighbours. We travel across Ukraine, and outdoors we come with a rain plan: a tent, a canopy or an indoor space nearby.

Who is responsible if a headphone is lost or damaged?

Our representative hands out and collects the headphones at one point at the entrance, so control on site is with us, and spare headphones come in the set as replacements. But the client is responsible for loss or damage caused by guests, regardless of which colleague caused it. The full agreed cost of the unit is compensated, not just the repair: this kind of equipment is hard to replace quickly in Ukraine. The terms are set out in the agreement before your order.

What can we organise at a corporate party instead of contests?

Silent disco: guests pick their own channel and volume, so nobody has to be brought on stage or persuaded. Anyone who wants to talk takes the headphones off around the neck, and the conversation goes on in a normal voice.

When should we book and what if plans change?

Our equipment is limited, so the most popular dates are taken in advance, especially in high season. A date is secured as soon as the prepayment arrives. If plans change, the date can be moved free of charge if you tell us at least 21 days ahead and the new date is free. Cancellation: 14+ days ahead we return the whole prepayment, 7–13 days ahead half of it, and less than 7 days ahead it is not returned.

What happens if an air-raid alert is declared?

An air-raid alert or a power cut is force majeure, and people’s safety matters more than the party. But our format is a big advantage here: it is very mobile. The headphones and the transmitter are easy to carry, so if there is a shelter nearby, such as a bomb shelter or a car park, the evening continues there: moving takes just a few minutes, and guests keep dancing safely. So the fear of having to leave the venue and ruin the evening goes away. The client is responsible for the overall organisation and safety of the event and the venue, and our representative on site has the right to stop the equipment if there is a real threat to people or equipment. If continuing is not possible, we first look for a new date; if moving is not possible, we agree on terms that take into account what has already actually been done.

How do we pay and is a contract needed?

The main way to pay is a bank transfer to the SILENT account by IBAN details: a 50% prepayment books the date and the other 50% is paid before the event. The agreement and order confirmation can be agreed electronically, by message or in a messenger, without meeting in person.

Do we need a DJ and who plays the music?

Not necessarily. We prepare three playlists in advance for the format and mood of the evening, so the music is already chosen and every guest switches between the three channels on their own. A live DJ is only needed if you want that particular DJ’s mix on site.

Does the format suit a New Year corporate party?

Yes, the format works in a hall, an office or a restaurant. Our equipment is limited, and the most popular dates, especially in high season, are taken in advance, so it is better to send a request early.
