Quiet Experiences

Silent disco is only one of the scenarios. Three channels of music in the headphones and quiet outside work wherever sound has to reach everyone but shouldn't fill the whole room: from a corporate party and a wedding to a museum tour, a conference and yoga.

Pick a theme below to see the matching formats, or send a request and we'll choose one together.

Runs and walks

Intimate concerts

Brand activations

Hands-on workshops

Simultaneous translation

One event — several languages. Everyone chooses their own channel and listens to the talk in a language they understand.

Inclusive events

Individual volume for people with sensory sensitivity, including autistic guests. Less overload.

Stand-up and comedy

Events for older guests

Bar tours

Quests

The Impostor game

Everyone hears the same music. One hears another. Watch who reacts oddly and find the traitor.

Dance competitions

Three teams. Three tracks. One dance floor. Each team hears its own music and doesn't lose the rhythm because of the others.

Hotels and resorts
